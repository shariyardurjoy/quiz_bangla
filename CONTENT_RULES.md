# QuizBangla Content Rules

The clue challenge is only fun when every question is fair and logically solvable.

## Question format

Each mystery must contain:
- exactly 1 answer
- exactly 4 distinct options
- exactly 3 clues
- a short explanation
- one category: Bangladesh Places, Food & Culture, Famous People, or Sports

## Clue quality rule

Every clue must be factually true for the answer. More importantly, **Clue 1 must already identify exactly one option among the four choices**. A player who knows the fact should have a fair chance to earn 100 points without revealing another clue.

Bad example:
> I am a district of Bangladesh.

If all four options are districts, the clue is unfair.

Better example:
> Among these choices, I am the district in Sylhet Division.

The later clues should confirm the answer with increasingly specific facts, not contradict or change the meaning of Clue 1.

## Distractor rules

- All four options should belong to a sensible comparable group.
- No two options may both satisfy the clue.
- Avoid joke or obviously impossible distractors.
- Avoid answer-position patterns; the app shuffles options at runtime.

## Fact rules

Prefer stable facts such as geography, heritage, literature, historic achievements, and established records. Avoid current office-holders, live rankings, temporary team roles, prices, schedules, or facts likely to become outdated quickly.

## Current bank

The app currently contains 100 curated mysteries:
- 25 Bangladesh Places
- 25 Food & Culture
- 25 Famous People
- 25 Sports

Each game randomly selects 10 mysteries, and answer options are shuffled for every round.
