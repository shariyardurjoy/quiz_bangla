# QuizBangla Content Rules

When adding clue questions:

1. The correct answer must appear exactly once in the four options.
2. Each clue must be factually true.
3. Even after only Clue 1, exactly one of the four options should satisfy the clue.
4. Clue 2 and Clue 3 should add confidence, not contradict or change the answer.
5. Avoid vague prompts such as “I am a district in Bangladesh” when several options are districts.
6. Avoid time-sensitive facts such as current office holders, current rankings, or live records unless they are intentionally updated.
7. Explanations should state why the answer is correct, not merely repeat the answer.
